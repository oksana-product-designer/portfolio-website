Oksana Popova
Senior Product Designer
8+ years exp. Remote / Ukraine
/ ABOUT

Despite my portfolio shows excessive experience in various areas I mainly specialize in B2B SaaS and GovTech domains where usability and user experience take the lead over marketing and sales. Working in complex, data-dense environments inspire me most. I advocate for design that accounts for user needs, technical constraints, business goals, and real-world context.

Beyond UX and design, I'm passionate about learning new things – exploring emerging technologies and AI to enhance my day-to-day work without compromising quality.

/ EXPERTISE INCLUDES
  • Planning and leading in-depth user research to uncover key insights
  • Crafting design artifacts – personas, journey maps, information architecture – to articulate research findings
  • Collaborating with cross-functional teams to achieve business goals and deliver high-quality products
  • Leveraging AI tools to optimize routine tasks and create interactive prototypes and MVPs
  • Developing and maintaining complex design systems to streamline product development
